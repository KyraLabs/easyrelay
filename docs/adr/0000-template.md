# NNNN. Title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by `NNNN` | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who

## Context

What forces this decision. The constraints, the facts, and what breaks if nothing is decided.
State what was verified rather than assumed, and where.

## Decision

What is being decided, in the active voice and in one or two sentences. Then the specifics.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it forecloses. Include the costs honestly —
a record that lists only benefits was written to justify a decision, not to make one.

## Alternatives considered

Each alternative, and the specific reason it lost. "It was worse" is not a reason.

## Revisit when

The condition that would make this decision worth reopening. Omit only if there is genuinely
no such condition.
